Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made by layering pasta sheets with rich sauces, cheese, and fillings such as meat or vegetables. It is known for its hearty texture, creamy layers, and golden, cheesy topping. 🍝

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced meat (or vegetables for a vegetarian version)
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Butter
  • All-purpose flour
  • Milk
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Oregano
  • Basil

Instructions

  1. Make the Meat Sauce
  2. Prepare the White Sauce (Béchamel)
  3. Cook or Prepare the Lasagna Sheets
  4. Assemble the Layers
  5. Bake the Lasagna
  6. Rest, Slice, and Serve
